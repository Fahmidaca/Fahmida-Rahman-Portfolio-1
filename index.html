Published IEEE · ACIIW 2026

Subject-Independent Multimodal Pain Classification via a Calibrated XGBoost and Domain-Adversarial Neural Network Ensemble

2026 14th International Conference on Affective Computing and Intelligent Interaction Workshops and Demos (ACIIW)

The task was to classify pain into three classes for people the model has never seen before, using four physiological signals: BVP, EDA, respiration and SpO₂.

  • An XGBoost classifier on engineered features, ensembled with a one-dimensional Domain-Adversarial Neural Network (DANN) trained on the raw signals.
  • Per-subject normalisation and domain-adversarial learning to generalise across individuals.
  • Calibrated soft voting to combine the two models.

2nd place in the AI4Pain 2026 Grand Challenge, held with ACII 2026, as part of Team BanglaBytes.
